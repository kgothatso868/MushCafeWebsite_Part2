## 1. Organisation overview
Mush Cafe is a warm, locally focused food business operating in Randburg, South Africa. The business is known for its grilled chicken meals, burgers, kotas, and combo meals. The brand is built around comfort, convenience, and satisfying local flavours that appeal to family and individual customers alike.

The café serves a community-driven audience that values affordable meals, quick takeaway options, and reliable quality. Mush Cafe’s identity is friendly, vibrant, and approachable, which makes it a strong fit for a modern digital presence that feels welcoming and easy to use.

## 2. Website goals and objectives
The goal of the Mush Cafe website is to create a professional and engaging online presence that supports the business’s marketing and customer communication needs. The website must help the café attract new customers while making it easier for existing customers to browse food options, contact the business, and send enquiries.

Key objectives:
- Increase local visibility for the brand
- Showcase menu items and prices clearly
- Improve online trust and professionalism
- Make customer contact and enquiry simple
- Ensure the site is mobile-friendly and easy to navigate
- Support conversions through clear calls to action

KPIs:
- Increased customer enquiries through the website
- Improved menu browsing engagement
- Higher brand recognition in the local area
- Better mobile user experience
- Clearer business information and location visibility

## 3. Current website analysis
Mush Cafe already has a strong business identity and customer appeal. The brand is associated with satisfying meals, quick service, and locally preferred food choices. The existing visual identity uses a warm brown and cream palette, food-focused imagery, and strong café branding, which aligns well with the intended audience.

The website can improve in the following areas:
- stronger mobile responsiveness across all pages
- better menu search and price sorting functionality
- clearer contact and enquiry user flow
- improved SEO metadata and browser optimisation
- more polished CSS styling and consistent visual hierarchy

## 4. Proposed website features and functionality
The final website will include the following key features:
- Homepage with hero section, introduction, and call to action
- Menu page with searchable and filterable dishes
- About page with mission, values, and brand story
- Contact page with business details, map, and contact form
- Enquiry page for product and service requests
- Food gallery with click-to-expand lightbox
- Responsive mobile navigation
- Form validation for contact and enquiry forms
- SEO metadata and sitemap support

## 5. Design and user experience
The design aesthetic will remain warm, approachable, and premium. The website will use:

- Colour scheme: warm brown, cream, caramel, and soft beige tones
- Typography: elegant serif headings paired with clean sans-serif body text
- Layout: clear content blocks, balanced spacing, rounded cards, and modern café styling
- User experience: easy navigation, readable content, strong CTA placement, and mobile-first behaviour

Low-fidelity wireframe ideas:
- Header with logo and navigation links
- Hero banner with branded message and action buttons
- Content cards or sections for menu and business highlights
- Contact cards and form layout
- Footer with key navigation and business details

## 6. Technical requirements
The project will use the following technologies:
- HTML5 for page structure
- CSS3 for styling and responsive layout
- JavaScript for menu interactivity, validation, and lightbox behaviour
- Semantic HTML for accessibility and structure
- Static file hosting for deployment without a framework
- SEO support through meta tags, robots instructions, and sitemap XML

## 8. Budget
This is a static website project, so the estimated costs are relatively low. The main costs include hosting, optional domain registration, and any paid assets if required. A free or low-cost deployment option is suitable.

Estimated budget:
- Hosting: R0–R150 per month depending on provider
- Domain: optional, varies by provider
- Assets and software: minimal or already included
- Maintenance: low-cost and manageable for a static website

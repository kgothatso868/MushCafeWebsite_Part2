# Mush Cafe Secondary Proposal

## Proposal 2: Local café growth and digital conversion strategy

### Organisation overview
This proposal explores Mush Cafe as a local food business that needs a stronger online presence to convert interest into sales and enquiries. The business already has a clear market identity through its menu offering, warm branding, and community appeal. The website will support this identity by improving the online customer journey and presenting the business as professional and reliable.

### Website goals and objectives
- Build brand trust for local customers
- Make menu browsing faster and easier
- Encourage direct enquiries and contact communication
- Highlight the café’s food quality and local value
- Improve accessibility and mobile usability
- Create a website that can be easily maintained and deployed

### Key website features
- Homepage with strong branding and clear calls to action
- Feature-based menu page with dynamic filtering and search
- Contact and enquiry forms for customer communication
- About section with story, values, and business positioning
- Map integration to improve local discoverability
- Social and external links to strengthen engagement

### Design and user experience
The design direction is focused on warmth, trust, and accessibility. The palette uses natural brown and cream tones, supported by golden accent colours to create a premium café feel without becoming too formal. The layout is structured to make content easy to scan, which suits a local food business looking to attract quick customer decisions.

### Technical requirements
- HTML5 structure
- CSS3 styling and responsive layout
- JavaScript interactivity for menu filtering and form validation
- Semantic structure for accessibility
- Static deployment on GitHub Pages or Netlify
- SEO metadata and sitemap support

### Timeline
- Planning and research
- HTML structure and page creation
- CSS styling and responsive refinements
- JavaScript functionality and validation
- SEO enhancements and documentation
- Final testing and deployment preparation

### Why this proposal works
This proposal is realistic, practical, and aligned with Mush Cafe’s needs. It focuses on improving the business’s digital presence without requiring a complex or expensive setup. The content, layout, and user journey are all compatible with a static website and meet PoE expectations for planning, interactivity, and business-focused design.
